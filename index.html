POLYCITY

AI Studio: ask the city a question, and watch Agent show the answer

Polycity is a city transport simulator. A planner describes a change to a city, such as a street closed, a fare raised or a bus line added, the platform simulates a whole day, and the planner reads what happened to traffic, transit, parking, exhaust and noise.

AI Studio is the mode of Polycity where Agent, Polycity's assistant, leads that work. The whole app becomes one talk. Every question the planner asks runs by itself as a work of its own, and beside the talk stands Agent's view: the map, the results or the draft Agent is talking about. Only Agent changes what the view shows. The planner asks, answers Agent's questions, and presses a place on the map to ask about it.

The nine films below are the running product, filmed at the pace a person reads, with a line at the foot of the screen saying what is happening. Agent's answers in them are real answers, given while the camera ran.

FILM 1

One talk, every question a work 1:58

A planner turns AI Studio on from the menu and reads the talk: what waits for them, what Agent is answering and what is finished.

  • AI Studio is one talk with Agent. Every question asked is a work in it, and the ask box stands at its foot.
  • Agent's view stands beside the talk from the start, opened on the newest page a work made.
  • The bar counts the works that wait for the planner, the ones being answered and the finished ones.
  • A question Agent stopped on is one card: the change drawn on its street, its facts in one strip, and its button.
  • Finished opens every finished work, twenty-five a page, each row naming what the work made.
  • The ask box names the model the next question leaves with and its effort: Quick, Balanced or Thorough.
  • Leave AI Studio shows the regular app on the same page. Turned on again, the talk stands as it did.

FILM 2

Agent's view beside the talk 2:01

A finished comparison is pressed, and the two runs it compared stand on the map beside its answer.

  • A pressed work opens with its whole answer. Beside it stands Compare, on the two runs the answer compared.
  • The view's head is Agent's mark and one strip naming what it shows.
  • The view holds the map, its colour key and the figures. It has no menu, no tool row, no run picker and no hours to press.
  • Another work pressed folds the comparison to one line: its question, the first words of its answer and its date.
  • One thing is the planner's to do in the view: press a place on the map. The place is ringed, and the ask box names it.

FILM 3

A figure, shown where it was read 1:34

An answer that gave one figure brings the run's Results into the view, on the measure the figure came from.

  • Pressing the work is enough. Agent's view stands on Results, where the figure was read.
  • A press on a card's name puts that card in the view, and the card is ringed for as long as the view shows it.
  • Leave AI Studio opens the same Results as the regular app shows it, with its menu, its tools and its run pickers.
  • Turned on again, AI Studio shows the work's view as Agent left it.

FILM 4

A question moves the view 2:13

The planner asks to see congestion at 17:00, then at 08:00, and presses nothing else.

  • With no work pressed, Agent's view holds what it last showed.
  • The question asks Agent to show something. Agent's own step opens the run's Results on Congestion at 17:00, while the answer is still being written.
  • Another hour takes another question, asked in the same work.
  • The view moves to 08:00 by Agent's step alone, and its head names the new hour.

FILM 5

Agent asks before it changes a draft 3:05

A street closure is asked for in words. Agent finds the street, shows the change dashed on the map, and waits for a yes.

  • The closure is asked for in words. The planner opens no editor and picks no street.
  • Agent asks first. Its view already stands on the editor's map of the scenario, moved to the street, with the closure dashed and Agent's ring on it.
  • The head and the key on the map both say what the dashes are: Not in the draft yet.
  • The question's card carries Add to draft, Change and Skip.
  • After the yes the dashes are gone, and the closure stands on the map as one of the draft's own changes.
  • Undo this answer, in the talk, takes the closure out of the draft again, and the map draws the draft as it was.

FILM 6

Four questions answered at once 2:39

Four questions are asked one after another, without waiting for an answer, and one line over the ask box follows all of them.

  • One line over the ask box carries all four questions as numbered marks, each at the step Agent has reached for it.
  • A press on a mark makes the sentence under the line speak for that question.
  • A fifth question is typed, and Send stands grey with its reason over it until one of the four has finished.
  • Each work finishes with its own answer, and the line leaves when the last answer has landed.
  • The bin asks first, naming the work and what goes with it.

FILM 7

A yes that waits for the planner 1:33

Agent asks first before it makes a file that others can open, and the question is still there when the planner comes back.

  • The export is asked for like any other question.
  • Agent stops on a question. On the line its mark is held at Answering, and the sentence reads Waiting for you.
  • The question's card asks before the file is made, with Export it, Change and Not yet.
  • Opened again later, the work still waits, with its question kept as one card.
  • The yes is given on the card. Agent takes the same work up again, and nothing asks a second time.
  • The work is finished with its file card.

FILM 8

Where traffic moved, ranked 1:52

A question about where traffic moved between two runs is answered with a ranked comparison, drawn on the map.

  • Agent's step opens Compare for the two runs in its view, while the answer is still being written.
  • The answer is a comparison card of ranked rows, each with this run's figure and the compared run's.
  • The card draws where its rows stand, each place with its outline.
  • A pressed place is ringed in the card, and the ask box names it for the next question.

FILM 9

Every look, and Bulgarian 4:03

The same talk and the same view under four of Polycity's looks, and then in Bulgarian.

  • The talk under Classic, Blocks, Route map and Lamplight: the bar's counts, the works and the ask box, with Agent's view held beside them.
  • Under each look, a pressed work with Compare in Agent's view.
  • In Bulgarian the bar, the talk and the ask box speak Bulgarian, and Agent is Агент.
  • Agent's view is named in Bulgarian too. Words a planner wrote, such as a work's question, stay as they were written.
